Male Cyber Fighters ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. It applies to the Male Cyber Fighters website (https://male-cyber-fighters.com and any associated domains), the installable mobile and desktop apps, and all related services (collectively, the "Service").

Male Cyber Fighters is a free, adults-only (18+) community for cyber wrestling and fight roleplay. We do not sell products or services, we do not process payments, and we do not show advertising. By using the Service you agree to this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service; if you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

Information We Collect

Account information. When you register we collect your username, email address, and password (stored only as a cryptographic hash — we never store plaintext passwords). You may optionally add a display name, age, bio and stats, preferred color and chat language, an avatar, extra profile photos, and physique details (height and weight, which the fight engine converts into attack/defense values for matches).

Content you create. We store the content you choose to post: public Arena messages, direct messages, custom-room messages and attachments, forum threads and replies, fight stories and their approvals, relationship (friend/partner) links, your block list, and reports you submit through the Report button.

Uploads. Images you upload (avatars, chat images, profile photos) are sent to ImgBB, which hosts the files; we store only the resulting links. GIFs and short video clips are stored on our own servers.

Security and activity logs. We automatically log your IP address, browser user-agent, and account security events (logins, failed logins, registrations, password changes and resets, account deletions). These logs are used only to operate and secure the Service and are viewable by site administrators in the admin panel.

Presence and session data. We track whether your account is currently online so features like live chat, calls, and unread badges work, and we store per-conversation read markers for your DMs.

Browser-side storage. Your browser stores your login session and fight-game state in local storage (for example the "currentUser" key and slash-command game data). This data stays on your device.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

• Operate the Service — authenticate you, display your profile on the roster, deliver messages, calls, rooms, forums, and stories, and run the fight engine.
• Translate public and room chat messages into each member's preferred language.
• Send essential transactional email, such as password-reset links.
• Enforce the Terms of Service and Server Rules — investigate reports, moderate content, and keep banned users out.
• Secure the Service — detect abuse, failed-login attacks, and ban evasion using our security logs.
• Improve and fix the Service.

Where Your Content Is Visible

Public areas — the Arena, Forums, Stories/Archives, the User Roster, and profile pages — are visible to anyone who visits the site. Anything you post there may be seen, copied, or shared by others.

Discord bridge. The Service is connected to our community Discord server. Messages you post in the public Arena are relayed to Discord, messages from Discord may appear in the Arena, and direct messages may be bridged to or from Discord. Reports you submit through the Report button are delivered to moderators, including via Discord. Content that crosses to Discord is also subject to Discord's own policies.

Private areas. DMs and private rooms are shown only to their participants, but they are stored on our servers, are not end-to-end encrypted, and can be reviewed by moderators when a report is filed. Audio calls connect peer-to-peer between participants; we route the connection setup but do not record call audio — though the other participant may be able to record on their own device.

Third Parties We Share With

We do not sell your personal information, and there are no advertisers, ad networks, or marketing trackers on the Service. We share information only with the providers needed to run it:

• Discord — message bridging, support reports, and our community server, as described above.
• Google Translate — the text of public and room messages is sent to Google's translation service so members can read chat in their own language.
• ImgBB — hosts the images you choose to upload (subject to ImgBB's own terms).
• Database and hosting providers — store and serve the Service's data.
• Email delivery provider — sends transactional email (password resets) to your registered address.
• Fight-engine services — the external move database and HP engine that power in-chat matches.

We may also disclose information where required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our members and the public.

Cookies & Browser Storage

The Service relies primarily on your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and to store session and game state; any cookies used are strictly functional (for example, those set by our hosting provider). We do not use advertising or third-party analytics cookies, and we never place personally identifiable information in cookies. You can block cookies or clear local storage in your browser, but sign-in and other features will stop working correctly.

Email

We send transactional email only — principally password-reset links and essential account or security notices. We do not send newsletters or marketing email, and there are no promotional email lists to unsubscribe from.

How Long We Keep Information

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to operate the Service, enforce our rules, and comply with legal obligations. When you delete your account we remove your profile and personal information from our active systems; content you shared with others (such as published stories naming another member, or messages already delivered to other members) may remain visible to them, and residual copies may persist briefly in backups and logs before being rotated out.

How We Protect Information

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures: passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes, password-reset tokens are stored as one-way hashes and expire after one hour, and traffic to the site is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information you transmit to us.

You can block other members and use the Report button to flag content or behavior.

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA/UK under GDPR, California under the CCPA, or Canada under PIPEDA), you may have additional rights — such as accessing a copy of your personal information, correcting it, or requesting deletion. To exercise any of these rights, email us at administrator@male-cyber-fighters.com; we will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights. We do not sell, and have never sold, personal information.
