    Public Chat Conduct Rules
    These rules apply to everyone. They’re designed to keep the space fun, competitive, and safe.
    ---

    1. Respect Comes First
    Even in a wrestling‑themed environment, respect is non‑negotiable.

    • Respect others — Treat every user like a real person with real feelings.
    • No harassment — Repeated targeting, insults, or attempts to provoke someone are not allowed.
    • No slurs or hate speech — Zero tolerance. Instant ban.
    • No personal attacks — Critique the character, not the person behind the screen.
    ---

    2. Banter vs Bullying
    Wrestling culture thrives on trash talk — but it must stay in‑character, mutual, and fun.

    ✔ Banter (Allowed)

    Banter is:
    • Mutual
    • Playful
    • In‑character
    • Not meant to harm
    • Not repeated after someone asks you to stop


    Examples:
    • “You’re stepping into the ring with the wrong guy.”
    • “I’ll break your streak tonight.”
    • “Nice try, rookie.”


    ✘ Bullying (Not Allowed)

    Bullying is:
    • Personal
    • Repeated
    • Intended to hurt
    • Targeting someone who isn’t engaging back
    • Bringing real‑life insults or trauma into the chat


    Examples:
    • “Nobody likes you here.”
    • “You’re pathetic.”
    • “You’re too stupid to be in this chat.”
    • Repeating insults after someone says “stop.”

    If someone says “stop,” “not cool,” “drop it,” or similar — the banter ends immediately.
    ---

    3. Do’s and Don’ts

    ✔ Do
    • Stay in character
    • Use playful rivalry
    • Keep competition fun
    • Respect boundaries
    • Report issues
    • Encourage new users
    • Keep cyber matches out of the public chat unless in designated areas

    ✘ Don’t
    • Don’t target individuals
    • Don’t spam or flood chat
    • Don’t impersonate staff
    • Don’t post private info
    • Don’t drag real‑life drama into chat
    • Don’t use threats, even jokingly

    ---

    4. Zero‑Tolerance Behaviors

    These result in immediate bans:
    • Hate speech
    • Threats of violence
    • Sharing private information
    • Sexual harassment
    • Targeted harassment
    • Encouraging self‑harm
    • Evading blocks or bans
    ---

    5. Conflict Resolution

    If a situation escalates:
    1. Stop engaging
    2. Block the user
    3. Report the behavior
    4. Moderators will review chat logs and act accordingly
    Blocking someone prevents them from DMing you, but moderators can still see interactions.
    ---

    6. Moderator Authority

    Moderators may:
    • Issue warnings
    • Mute users
    • Remove messages
    • Ban users
    • Lock chat temporarily

    Moderator decisions are final.
